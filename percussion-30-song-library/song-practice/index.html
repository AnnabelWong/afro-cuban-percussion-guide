    Your listening task

    Guidance: how to listen
    1. Listen once without naming instruments. Notice dry, ringing, rattling and scraping sounds.
    2. Choose one voice. Follow it for several repetitions while keeping a comfortable pulse.
    3. Replay and compare with an instrument demonstration. Treat an uncertain name as a hypothesis.
    4. Note a timestamp and the recurring figure. Avoid assigning a clave direction unless you can locate the phrase and pattern.

    Congas and bongó can both produce bright slaps; pitch alone is insufficient. Bells ring, shell strokes tend to be drier, and scraped or shaken textures can overlap in a full mix.
